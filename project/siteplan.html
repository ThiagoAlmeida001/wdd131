Flamengo Fan Site Planning Document

WDD 131 - Dynamic Web Fundamentals

Site Name

Nação Rubro-Negra: The Flamengo Hub

Reason for choosing: I chose this subject because Flamengo is my favorite football team and the team of my heart. I have supported Flamengo since I was a child, and I enjoy sharing its history and passion with other people.

Site Purpose

My website will introduce Clube de Regatas do Flamengo. It will include the club's history, its greatest achievements, famous players, the Maracanã Stadium, and a gallery of photos. It will also have a section about the current team and interesting facts for fans.

Scenarios

Here are two questions that a typical visitor might ask when exploring this website:

  • Scenario 1: Where can I find a summary of Flamengo's greatest historical achievements and trophies?
  • Scenario 2: Who are the legendary players that shaped the club's history, and who is on the current roster?

Color Schema

The primary colors reflect the official identities of the club (Rubro-Negro) alongside clean neutral tones for readability:

  • Primary Color (Red): #C4122C - Used for main headings, navigation bars, and structural highlights.
  • Secondary Color (Black): #111111 - Used for headers, footers, and prominent text accents.
  • Accent Color (Gold/Yellow): #F1C40F - Used for special call-outs, hover effects, or highlighting trophy milestones.
  • Background Color: #FFFFFF - Clean white text area backgrounds to keep the website modern and legible.

Typography

We will use a combination of two web fonts to establish a strong hierarchical design:

  • Heading Font: Oswald (or sans-serif fallback) - A bold, condensed font ideal for powerful sports headings and titles.
  • Body Font: Roboto (or Arial, sans-serif fallback) - A highly readable, clean geometric font used for all main articles, descriptions, and paragraphs.
